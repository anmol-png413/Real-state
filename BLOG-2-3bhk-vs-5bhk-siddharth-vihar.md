## Investment Perspective: Which Makes More Financial Sense?

### Rental Demand

The 3 BHK segment has broader rental demand. Ghaziabad's growing professional class — employed in Noida's tech parks, Sahibabad's industrial belt, and Delhi's commercial hubs — is the natural renter for a 3 BHK at ₹22,000–28,000/month. Finding tenants will be faster.

The 5 BHK commands higher rent in absolute terms but has a narrower renter profile. HNI renters, corporate leases, and senior executives are the likely tenants. These tenants are fewer but typically sign longer leases and maintain properties better.

### Appreciation

On a percentage basis, 3 BHK and 5 BHK tend to appreciate similarly in the early years of a project. However, scarcity plays a major role at possession. With fewer 5 BHK units in the project and virtually no comparable inventory in the micro-market, the 5 BHK has a stronger scarcity argument for appreciation post-2029.

### Liquidity

If you need to exit, a 3 BHK sells faster. The buyer universe for ₹1.2–1.4 Cr flats in Siddharth Vihar is significantly larger than for ₹2+ Cr units. For investors who may want to exit within 2–3 years of possession, the 3 BHK carries lower liquidity risk.
